Execution Roadmap — 12 weeks

Volatility playbook

StatePosture
CalmNormal capped cadence; rediscovery, lessons, replay.
ChoppyLower promo frequency; volatility/risk education.
StressPause promotions; service messages + calm explainers only. No “buy the dip”.

Thresholds (VIX/drawdown) are proposals for Risk/Research to approve. Banned copy: “last chance”, “don’t miss the dip”, “guaranteed”, “safe returns”, “earn ₹X”, guilt/shame, return screenshots, leaderboards.

Compliance non-negotiables

No return promises · no advice in marketing · no activation inducements · no live-price game/P&L leaderboard · no F&O-first activation · no dark patterns · consent & purpose limitation · exclude fraud/regulator-deactivated accounts · show costs & risks · stress-state pause. Replay Lab ships lesson-only until data-rights + Legal/Compliance sign-off.
